# Product Care & FAQ

> Synthetic demo content. Halden & Co. is a fictional home-goods store; products are invented for the SupportIQ demo.

## Lumen Brass Table Lamp

The Lumen lamp uses a standard E26 bulb base and is rated for LED bulbs up to 9 watts (a 60-watt incandescent equivalent). Do not use incandescent bulbs above 40 watts. The lamp includes a 1.8 metre fabric cord with an inline dimmer switch. The brass finish is unlacquered and will develop a natural patina; polish with a dry soft cloth only.

## Morrow Oak Side Table

The Morrow side table is solid white oak with a hard-wax oil finish. It measures 45 cm wide, 45 cm deep and 55 cm tall, and supports up to 20 kg. Assembly takes about 10 minutes and the legs attach with the included hex key. Wipe spills promptly; re-oil once a year with a hard-wax oil.

## Fjord Wool Throw

The Fjord throw is 100% New Zealand lambswool, 130 by 180 cm. Hand wash cold with wool detergent or dry clean. Do not tumble dry. Some shedding during the first few uses is normal.

## Arden Linen Duvet Cover

The Arden duvet cover is stonewashed European flax linen with coconut-shell buttons. Machine wash at 40°C on a gentle cycle and tumble dry low. Linen softens with every wash. Available in Queen and King sizes.

## Tidal Stoneware Dinner Set

The Tidal set includes four dinner plates, four side plates and four bowls in reactive-glaze stoneware. It is dishwasher and microwave safe, but not oven safe. Because the glaze is reactive, each piece varies slightly in colour; this is not a defect.

## Kestrel Ceramic Pour-Over Set

The Kestrel set includes a ceramic dripper, a 600 ml carafe and 40 paper filters. It fits standard size 02 cone filters. The carafe is not suitable for stovetop use.
