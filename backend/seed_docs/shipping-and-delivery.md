# Shipping & Delivery Guide

> Synthetic demo content. Halden & Co. is a fictional home-goods store created for the SupportIQ demo.

## Processing time

Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1 to 2 business days. Large furniture items ship separately and can take up to 4 business days to leave our warehouse.

## Delivery estimates

Standard shipping within the United States takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Expedited shipping takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch. Standard shipping is free on orders over $75; otherwise it costs $6.95.

## Tracking

A shipping confirmation email with a tracking link is sent as soon as the carrier scans the parcel. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after the first scan. Orders with several items may arrive in separate parcels with separate tracking links.

## International shipping

We currently ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Import duties and taxes are calculated at checkout for the UK, EU and Australia; Canadian orders may be charged duties by the carrier on delivery. We do not currently ship to other countries.

## Changing a delivery address

The delivery address can be changed only before the order ships. After dispatch, the customer can request a redirect directly with the carrier using the tracking link, but we cannot guarantee the carrier will accept it.

## Delayed or missing parcels

If tracking has not updated for more than 5 business days, or a parcel is marked delivered but the customer cannot find it, ask the customer to check with neighbours and their building's mail room first. Missing-parcel cases follow RR-5 in the Returns & Refunds Policy.
