# Billing & Payments FAQ

> Synthetic demo content. Halden & Co. is a fictional home-goods store created for the SupportIQ demo.

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Halden gift cards. Payment is authorised when the order is placed and captured when the order ships.

## Duplicate or pending charges

If a customer sees two charges for one order, one of them is usually a pending authorisation. Pending authorisations that were not captured drop off automatically within 3 to 5 business days, depending on the bank. If both charges are still showing as posted (not pending) after 5 business days, the support team should escalate to the billing team with the order number and the last four digits of the card.

## Invoices and receipts

An itemised receipt is emailed when the order ships. Customers who need a VAT invoice or a business invoice can request one from support and it will be issued within 2 business days.

## Price adjustments

If an item the customer bought goes on sale within 14 days of their order date, we will refund the difference once on request. Price adjustments do not apply to clearance or final-sale events.

## Gift cards

Gift cards never expire and cannot be exchanged for cash, except where required by law.
