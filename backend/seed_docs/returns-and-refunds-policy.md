# Returns & Refunds Policy

> Synthetic demo content. Halden & Co. is a fictional home-goods store created for the SupportIQ demo.

## RR-1 Standard return window

Unused items in their original condition can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. The return window is counted from the delivery date shown in the carrier tracking, not the order date. Items that have been used, washed or assembled cannot be returned unless they are damaged or defective.

## RR-2 Final-sale items

Items marked as final sale at checkout cannot be returned or exchanged. The only exception is a final-sale item that arrives damaged or defective, which is handled under the damaged items policy (RR-3).

## RR-3 Damaged or defective on arrival

If an item arrives damaged or defective, the customer must report it within 14 days of delivery. Please ask the customer for a photo of the damage and of the shipping box. Eligible customers can choose a free replacement or a full refund. Customers are never charged return shipping for damaged or defective items, and for low-value items we may not ask for the item back at all.

## RR-4 Cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund at any time before they ship. Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery under the standard return window.

## RR-5 Orders that have not arrived

Most orders arrive within the estimated delivery window shown in the shipping confirmation. If an order is still not delivered 14 days after it was placed, the support team opens a lost-package investigation with the carrier. Refunds or replacements for lost packages are issued once the investigation concludes, which usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

## RR-6 How refunds are issued

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once a returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse, the refund is issued within 2 business days, and the customer's bank typically takes 5 to 7 business days to show it. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error or a damaged item. Gift card purchases are refunded as store credit.

## RR-7 Approval limits

Support agents may approve refunds up to $300. Any refund above $300 must be approved by a support team lead before it is processed. Refunds outside the policy (for example, outside the return window) are exceptions and always require a team lead's decision.

## RR-8 Halden Plus members

Customers with a Halden Plus membership have an extended return window of 45 days from delivery. All other conditions of this policy still apply.

## Return shipping

For change-of-mind returns we email a prepaid return label, and a return shipping fee of $7.95 is deducted from the refund. Returns must be dropped off within 10 days of the label being issued.
