# Support Escalation Guidelines (internal)

> Synthetic demo content written for the SupportIQ demo workspace.

## When to escalate to a team lead

Escalate a ticket to a team lead when the customer mentions legal action, a lawyer or a chargeback; when a product may have caused injury, fire, smoke or electric shock; when a refund above $300 is requested; or when the customer asks for an exception to policy.

## Safety reports

Any report of a product causing injury, fire, smoke or electric shock must be escalated the same day. Ask the customer to stop using the product immediately. Do not speculate about the cause and do not admit liability in writing.

## Privacy requests

Requests to delete or export personal data are forwarded to the privacy team, which responds within 30 days. Support agents should confirm receipt but must not delete accounts themselves.

## Account access

Customers can reset their password from the sign-in page using the "Forgot password" link. For security, support agents cannot change a customer's email address or password on their behalf.

## Tone

Acknowledge the customer's situation before explaining policy. Never blame the customer, and never promise outcomes that require approval before that approval is given.
