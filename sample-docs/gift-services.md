## Gift wrapping

Gift wrapping is available on all items except furniture for $6 per item. Wrapped items ship with a handwritten card; the message can be up to 200 characters. Prices are never shown on the packing slip of a gift-wrapped order.

## Engraving

The Lumen Brass Table Lamp base and the Kestrel Ceramic Pour-Over carafe can be engraved with up to 24 characters for $15. Linen and wool products, including the Arden Linen Duvet Cover, cannot be engraved, but they can be monogrammed with up to 3 embroidered initials for $12. Engraved and monogrammed items are made to order, ship 3 business days later than usual, and are final sale unless they arrive damaged.

## Changing a gift message

A gift message or engraving text can be changed within 2 hours of placing the order by contacting support. After that the item is already in production.
